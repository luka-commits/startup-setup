What this folder actually is

Four jobs in one folder. The combination is the system.

One folder on your machine
projects · context · reference · inbox
It organises your documents
What came in, what you are making, what went out, dated. Every project the same shape.
It is the data basis
One place all the answers come from, instead of whatever you pasted into a chat.
It keeps status and to-dos
What is open, what is waiting on whom, what was decided and why.
It is connected to what you run
Mailbox, calendar, storage, CRM, your code. It reads them where they are.
AND OUT OF ALL FOUR
It briefs you every morning, from the same folder, without you assembling anything.
Any one of these on its own is just a folder, or just an integration. Together they are the thing that can answer "where does everything stand".
Not a tool you visit. The place your work is described, so anything can act on it.
