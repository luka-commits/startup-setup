Four folders, and each one answers a different question.

context/
how does everything stand?
Your projects, your open tasks, the history of decisions. Read at the start of every session, this is the memory.
projects/
what am I working on?
One folder per project, and inside it always the same four.
inputs/
what came in, never edited
work/
what you are making
outputs/
what went out, dated
code/
the project's code, with its own history
same four in every project, nothing to decide
reference/
how does this work?
The things that do not change: rules, tool manuals, scripts.
inbox/
what have I not looked at yet?
The drop zone, emptied as you go.
the rule behind it
State lives centrally, material lives with its project. No fact in two places.
You never have to decide where something goes. The question it answers decides.
