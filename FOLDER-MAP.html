Your workspace: the folder map

Every file has exactly one job. This map shows what lives where and which part of it belongs to you.

🟢 State: changes daily 🟡 Rules & knowledge: rarely changed, but your adjustment screw 🔵 Material: what comes in and goes out ⚙️ System: arrives fresh with updates, don't touch

The 6 files in the root interchangeable

SETUP.mdThe installation: from an empty machine to the first briefing
ONBOARDING.mdYour entry point: first start, first steps
README.mdWhat this package is, in two minutes
WHAT-THIS-SYSTEM-DOES.mdWhat the system reads and what it never does, also for compliance questions
VERSION.mdVersion number and contact person if something doesn't work
CLAUDE.mdOperating manual for Claude itself: the AI reads it, not you

.claude/ interchangeable

The machinery: the commands /morning, /eod, /ingest, /email, /setup and the permissions. Hidden folder, comes with the repo. You change nothing here; requests for adjustments go to the contact person in VERSION.md.

context/ YOURS

The state of your working life. Maintained automatically by the commands. But you can look in at any time or have it changed in the chat.

config.yamlWho you are: name, email, location, office days, working language (filled by /setup)
PROJECTS.mdYour projects: purpose, status, stakeholders. No tasks.
STATUS.mdYour tasks, the single to-do truth in the system
JOURNAL.mdWhat happened, what was decided, newest entries at the top
PERSONAL.mdNon-project things (goals, notes to yourself)
EMAIL_STYLE.mdYour mail style, which /setup derives from your sent mails
today.htmlThe dashboard that /morning rebuilds every day

projects/ YOURS

One folder per case or initiative, including the filed original documents. _template/ is the template for new ones, _archive/ the storage for finished ones. Nothing is ever deleted.

inbox/ YOURS

The drop zone: just put transcripts, PDFs and notes here and say /ingest. Processed items without a project link end up in processed/, your daily briefings move to archive/ automatically after 14 days.

_tmp/ nothing of yours

This is where Claude puts the short scripts it uses to open mail drafts or build the dashboard. Always the same file names, overwritten on every run — never more than one per purpose is left lying around. Nothing of yours belongs in here.

reference/ adjustable

Rules and knowledge that the commands read. Changes rarely. But this is exactly where you tune the system to yourself without touching the machinery.

mail-triage-rules.mdHow /morning sorts your mails. Stricter or looser? Change it here.
mcp.mdConnectors for mail, calendar and storage: what they can do, what they never do
tools.mdThe two recommended tools: firecrawl (web content) and playwright (browser)
plugins.mdOptional extensions, allowed and described, but not necessary
scripts/Small helpers that the system files away itself

Three rules that hold it all together

  • No fact lives in two places. Tasks are in STATUS.md, project states in PROJECTS.md, history in the JOURNAL, never twice.
  • Nothing of yours is deleted behind your back. "Gone" means archive folder, and you can always go back. Real deletion happens only when you ask for it, and you get asked once before it does.
  • With a new version you only take the green and blue along: context/, projects/, inbox/ (and your own additions in reference/). The rest arrives fresh.

This map describes the fully set-up state. On the very first start, /setup walks you through the setup: just open Claude Code in this folder and start writing.
